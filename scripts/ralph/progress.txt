## Codebase Patterns
- READ FIRST each iteration: memoria.md (root) + the relevant *_RULES.md for the area you touch (API_RULES.md, DB_RULES.md, FRONTEND_RULES.md, AI_RULES.md). memoria.md §10 changelog has the latest state.
